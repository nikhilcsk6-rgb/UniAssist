EXAMINATION POLICY
Each semester has two internal assessments of 25 marks each and one end-semester examination of 100 marks. A student must have at least 75 percent attendance to be eligible to write the end-semester examination. Students with attendance between 65 and 75 percent may apply for condonation with a medical certificate and a fee of Rs. 500.

Odd-semester examinations are held in December and even-semester examinations in May. The timetable is published at least 21 days before the first examination. Hall tickets are issued 7 days before the examination and must be carried to every paper.

Revaluation: applications must be made within 10 days of the result announcement at a fee of Rs. 800 per paper. The minimum passing mark is 40 percent in each paper and 50 percent in aggregate.
