ADMISSIONS
The MCA programme is a two-year postgraduate programme. Eligibility: a bachelor's degree with Mathematics at the 10+2 level or at graduation level, with a minimum of 50 percent aggregate marks (45 percent for reserved categories). Admission is based on the state entrance test score. The application window opens on 1 May and closes on 30 June each year.

FEE STRUCTURE
The MCA tuition fee is Rs. 60,000 per year, payable in two instalments: 50 percent at admission and 50 percent before the start of the third semester. The examination fee is Rs. 2,500 per semester. The hostel fee is Rs. 45,000 per year including mess charges. A late payment fine of Rs. 100 per day applies after the due date.

SCHOLARSHIPS
The Merit Scholarship gives a 25 percent tuition waiver to students scoring above 85 percent in the entrance test. The Need-Based Grant covers up to 50 percent of tuition for students whose family income is below Rs. 3,00,000 per year. Applications for scholarships must be submitted to the Student Welfare Office within 30 days of admission.
